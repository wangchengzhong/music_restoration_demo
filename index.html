Generative Music Restoration with High-Fidelity Acoustic Latents and Content Representations

  • Chengzhong Wang1,2

* Corresponding author

This demo accompanies our work on restoring music degraded by loudspeaker playback and everyday recording. The examples include severely attenuated or missing low-frequency bands and ultra-low signal-to-noise ratios (SNRs). In these challenging conditions, the proposed method preserves observable acoustic detail and reconstructs missing musical components with high fidelity, while the compared methods can produce substantial hallucinations.

Our system combines a high-fidelity acoustic latent space built from PupuJEPA-Tiny and decoded by Vocos with RePupu, a PupuJEPA-Base encoder-predictor that restores content representations. Together, the degraded acoustic latents and restored content condition a three-step Pixel MeanFlow generator.
